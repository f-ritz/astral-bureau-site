03 / ABOUT THE PRACTICE

Curiosity, with
a little rigor.

The Astral Bureau is an independent esoteric consultancy interested in more than quick horoscope labels. Its practice brings together traditional playing-card divination, Western astrology, Chinese astrology, and numerology.

It is an invitation to investigate meaningful questions, examine recurring themes, and come away with a clearer account of the interpretation—even when the answer is complicated.

Readings are interpretive and reflective. They do not replace medical, legal, financial, or mental-health advice, and they do not guarantee an outcome.

04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you
begin.

What information does a reading require?+

Numerology typically uses your date of birth. Astrology benefits from the date, birthplace, and accurate birth time; some chart features cannot be established without a time. Cartomancy starts with a question or topic.

Is a reading written or live?+

Most introductory offerings are written. The live cartomancy consultation is a 30-minute session, with video preferred. Details will be confirmed when booking opens.

Will a reading tell me exactly what will happen?+

No. These systems offer symbolic interpretations, perspectives, and possible themes. You remain responsible for your decisions, and outcomes are never guaranteed.

How do I book or pay?+

Online booking and payment have not opened yet. This website currently publishes the service catalogue only; it does not collect orders, payments, or birth details.

What about privacy?+

This version of the site does not ask you to submit personal birth information. Before orders open, the Bureau will publish a privacy notice explaining how client details are handled.
