Privacy Notice

How this pre-launch website handles visitor information and what will change before client intake begins.

Published November 26, 2026Pre-launch website policy
Current status: The Bureau is publishing a service catalogue only. This site has no booking form, client account, checkout, or payment collection. The terms for any future paid service will be disclosed and agreed upon before an order is accepted.

1. Who operates this website

This site is published under the trade name The Astral Bureau, an independent esoteric consultancy being prepared for launch in the United States. The operator's formal business identity and a dedicated privacy contact channel will be published before client information is accepted. Until then, please do not send birth information or other personal details to the Bureau through unsolicited channels.

2. Information handled by this site today

This is currently a static informational website. It does not offer forms, user accounts, newsletter signups, appointments, payments, or a way to upload birth charts. No client-intake database is connected to the website.

Technical data may still be processed when you visit: your IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, approximate network location, request headers, and security signals. These are used by the site's hosting and security infrastructure to deliver pages, address abuse, and maintain service reliability.

3. Cloudflare hosting and cookies

The website is hosted through Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical request data and may set strictly necessary security or bot-protection cookies (for example, challenge or clearance cookies) when relevant. Cloudflare's own privacy policy and cookie policy describe its processing and cookies.

The site's current HTML and CSS do not include advertising pixels, third-party analytics scripts, or a marketing newsletter tracker. Cloudflare infrastructure may nevertheless produce traffic or security statistics. If optional analytics or advertising tools are added later, this notice and any necessary consent controls will be updated before deployment.

4. What we do not currently collect

We do not currently collect your name, date or time of birth, birthplace, relationship questions, card-spread details, messages, or payment information through this site. Future readings will require a separate, clearly explained intake process.

5. Intended future client-information policy

Before booking opens, we will publish a revised notice identifying the legal business operator, contact information, categories of data needed for each service, processing purposes, providers, data retention periods, security practices, applicable privacy rights, and the process for requests and complaints.

The intended principle is to request only details relevant to the reading ordered. Birth information and personal questions are not to be repurposed for social-media monitoring, investigations, unsolicited marketing, or unrelated personal research.

Clients requesting readings involving another identifiable person should submit that person's private information only where they have an appropriate lawful basis or permission to share it. We will not invite the submission of non-public information about unwilling third parties.

6. Disclosure, security, and retention

At present, the site relies on Cloudflare to deliver and protect its static pages. We do not sell personal information or share information gathered by an intake form, because no such form exists. Hosting and security records are handled according to the relevant provider's systems and retention arrangements; we cannot promise that every technical request record is immediately deleted.

Before paid services launch, client records will require an access-control plan, secure storage, a defined retention and deletion schedule, and a way to respond to lawful privacy requests. The presence of this notice does not itself create those systems.

7. Children

Future paid readings are intended for adults aged 18 and older. The current site is informational, is not directed at children, and does not intentionally seek personal information from them.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use browser controls to manage cookies, subject to any essential security functions. Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights relating to personal data. A verified privacy-request channel will be provided before the Bureau collects client data; this website does not currently provide a client-information request portal.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated as the business adds booking, payment, communication, or analytics systems. Material changes will be reflected in the published effective date. We will not quietly expand collection practices while continuing to represent this as a static catalogue.
