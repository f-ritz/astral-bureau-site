THE ASTRAL BUREAU / LEGAL INFORMATION

Services & Disclaimers

Interpretive scope, client boundaries, and the refund policy status before bookings open.

Published November 26, 2026Pre-launch website policy
Current status: The Bureau is publishing a service catalogue only. This site has no booking form, client account, checkout, or payment collection. The terms for any future paid service will be disclosed and agreed upon before an order is accepted.

1. Interpretive practice, not guaranteed outcomes

The Astral Bureau offers a perspective through Western astrology, Chinese astrology, numerology, and traditional playing-card cartomancy. These practices are not evidence-based methods of predicting particular events or verifying another person's feelings. Interpretations are symbolic, contextual, and subject to uncertainty.

2. No substitute for professional services

No reading should be treated as medical diagnosis or treatment, mental-health care, legal counsel, investment guidance, financial planning, or safety advice. Do not postpone necessary professional care or make high-stakes decisions solely on the basis of a reading.

3. Personal autonomy and third parties

A reading cannot establish consent, override a person's decisions, or guarantee romantic reconciliation. The Bureau will not frame a third party as obligated to love, contact, date, marry, or otherwise engage with a client. When a request involves another person, submit only information you are entitled to share.

4. Current booking and payment status

Booking is not yet open. No payment is currently accepted through this website. The prices shown on the catalogue are introductory service previews, not active checkout offers. Do not send money or sensitive personal details in an attempt to order a reading.

5. Refund and cancellation policy — pending launch

Because this website currently accepts no orders, there are no website purchases to cancel or refund. Before sales begin, the Bureau will publish and display at checkout a specific policy addressing:

  • When a written reading enters preparation and the estimated delivery window.
  • How and when an order or appointment can be canceled or rescheduled.
  • Refund eligibility before preparation, for non-delivery, and where a service materially differs from its description.
  • How a client requests a refund and the expected processing method and time.
  • Any additional rights provided by applicable consumer-protection law.

A final refund policy has not yet been adopted. No statement on this page represents a blanket “no refunds” policy or waives rights that customers may have under law.

6. Service scope and client information

Written readings may involve birth dates, birthplaces, and optional birth times. A missing birth time can limit astrological interpretations. Cartomancy requires a question or intended theme. Specific intake fields, timelines, and retention rules will be disclosed before accepting client submissions.

7. Eligibility and boundaries

Planned paid services are intended for adults aged 18 and older. The Bureau may decline an order when it falls outside the published scope, asks for unlawful conduct, or cannot be completed responsibly. Any payment, if accepted in the future, will be handled according to the final disclosed cancellation and refund rules.

8. Before the first order

The Bureau must first publish an operator identity and contact information, final service terms, a complete client privacy policy, a secure order workflow, and a clear refund/cancellation policy. Until those items are in place, this is a service information site—not a transactional storefront.
